# Named Gwent decks for the gwent_duel scenario. run_test.py replaces a name in W3T_GWENT_DECKS by its card list.
# One deck per line: "name: leader+card+card+...", card names as in GetCardDefs/GetLeaderDefs (the game's
# def_gwint_*_cards.xml). A card may repeat. No special card (the scenario's inline decks have none).
#
# plain_*: unit cards without abilities that ask a question (no medic, spy, decoy, scorch, weather, horn, agile, muster)
#   and a leader whose ability has no choice, so no dialog opens during the match.
# medic_nr: Northern Realms with medics (dialogs from Task 12).
# leaders_nilf: Nilfgaard with a leader whose ability asks a choice (emhyr_gold: resurrect from the enemy graveyard).
# agile_medic: a medic and agile cards (the medic's row choice on agile resurrects, spec 7.3).
#
# Built-in deck names (W3T_GWENT_DECKS=North,...) come from def_gwint_decks.xml through GetDeck(name, 1): North and
# NilfEasy hold cards, while Nilf, nml, scotial and ske are the player's starter decks with a leader and no cards.
