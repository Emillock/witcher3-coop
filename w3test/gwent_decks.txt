# Named Gwent decks for the gwent_duel scenario. run_test.py replaces a name in W3T_GWENT_DECKS by its card list.
# One deck per line: "name: leader+card+card+...", card names as in GetCardDefs/GetLeaderDefs (the game's
# def_gwint_*_cards.xml). A card may repeat. No special card (the scenario's inline decks have none).
#
# plain_*: unit cards without abilities that ask a question (no medic, spy, decoy, scorch, weather, horn, agile, muster)
#   and a leader whose ability has no choice, so no dialog opens during the match.
# medic_nr: Northern Realms with medics: the medic's pick is a decision (D NUR), except against an Invader of the North
#   leader (invader_nilf), where non-hero medics resurrect at random (the hero medic yennefer still asks).
# leaders_nilf: Nilfgaard with a leader whose ability asks a choice (emhyr_gold: resurrect from the enemy graveyard,
#   D LRES when the enemy graveyard holds more than one target).
# agile_medic: a medic and agile cards (the medic's row choice on agile resurrects, spec 7.3); its only medic is the hero
#   yennefer, whose pick is always a decision (D NUR).
# invader_nilf: Nilfgaard with Invader of the North (emhyr_platinium: every non-hero medic of both decks and the
#   resurrect leaders pick at random), non-hero medics and the agile olgierd (the medic's row choice, spec 7.3).
# res_nml: Monsters with a resurrect leader (eredin_silver: D LRES with more than one target, random against
#   invader_nilf).
# crach_ske: Skellige with Crach an Craite (shuffles both graveyards into the decks, no choice).
# tricks_nr, tricks_nilf: plain units plus decoys, scorch, the four weather cards and a horn, so moves onto a card
#   (decoy: C), global moves (scorch: G), weather plays (P to the weather slot, seat N) and horns go over the wire;
#   none of them asks a choice.
# weather_nml: Monsters with eredin_bronze (pick a weather card from the deck: D LWEA, since the deck holds three
#   different weather cards).
# bin_nml: Monsters with eredin_gold (discard two hand cards, then take one from the deck: two D LBIN and one D LPICK).
#   The vanilla AI never plays this leader (its AI value is -1), so autoplay does not reach these decisions by itself.
# view_nilf: Nilfgaard with emhyr_bronze (shows three of the opponent's hand cards on the leader owner's board only;
#   nothing is sent).
#
# Built-in deck names (W3T_GWENT_DECKS=North,...) come from def_gwint_decks.xml through GetDeck(name, 1): North and
# NilfEasy hold cards, while Nilf, nml, scotial and ske are the player's starter decks with a leader and no cards.
